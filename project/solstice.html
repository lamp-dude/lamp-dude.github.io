The world is ending in five minutes.

In SOLSTICE, you wake up to news of an unexplained catastrophe and step outside to spend your final moments however you choose. Explore the world and meet people who are each facing the end in their own way. This game was created during my time at CMU’s NHSGA summer program. During my time there, I learned plenty about brainstorming with a team. My role was a programmer, mainly working on the core gameplay mechanics, lighting, and level design. Made with Unity3D URP.
